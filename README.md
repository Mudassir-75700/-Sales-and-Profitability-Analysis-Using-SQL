**📌 Project Overview**
This project focuses on analyzing transactional sales data using MySQL to uncover insights related to sales performance, profitability, customer behavior, product performance, regional trends, and discount impact.

The project demonstrates the practical application of SQL for transforming raw sales data into meaningful business insights and supports data-driven decision-making.

**🎯 Project Objectives**

The primary objectives of this project were to:

Analyze overall sales and profitability
Identify top-performing products and sub-categories
Evaluate regional and country-level performance
Analyze customer purchasing behavior
Identify repeat customers
Track monthly sales trends
Calculate cumulative sales
Analyze the impact of discounts on profitability
Identify loss-making orders
Compare category-wise sales and profit margins
Apply advanced SQL techniques for ranking and segmentation

**🔍 Business Analysis Performed**

**1. Sales Analysis**
Calculated total sales
Analyzed monthly sales trends
Calculated daily sales and running sales totals
Identified top-selling products and sub-categories

**2. Profitability Analysis**
Identified the region with the highest profit
Analyzed category-wise profit
Calculated category-level profit ratios
Identified the most profitable sub-category in each region
Identified orders generating negative profit

**3. Customer Analysis**
Ranked customers based on revenue
Identified repeat customers based on order frequency
Analyzed customer contribution to overall sales

**4. Product Analysis**
Identified products with the highest quantity sold
Identified the Top 3 products within each category
Compared product-level sales performance

**5. Regional & Country Analysis**
Compared sales across regions
Ranked countries based on total sales
Identified the most profitable sub-category within each region
